Slide Booklet Maker

Turn a slide PDF into a portrait note-taking booklet: title cover, then 2 slides per page with 3 note lines under each.

Drop the slide PDF here or click to choose. Export it from PowerPoint first (File > Export > PDF).

US Letter, light gray pages, white note area with 3 lines under each slide, page numbers in a teal band. Everything runs in your browser; the file never leaves your computer.
